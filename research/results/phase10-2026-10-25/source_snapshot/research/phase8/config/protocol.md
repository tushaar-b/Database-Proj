# Phase 8 exploratory protocol — fixed before revised-policy outcomes

All 2022–2025 and 2026 YTD periods have previously been inspected. They are reused exploratory periods, NOT untouched holdouts. No new independently validated historical universe is available locally. No performance-driven parameter fitting is planned. Primary: 2022–2025; robustness: 2022–2023, 2024–2025, 2026 YTD ending 2026-10-22. Existing verified/restricted 40-security dataset, 57,400 rows, same nine synthetic profiles, INR 100,000 starting brokerage cash, reserve-protected monthly contributions, original rank/momentum inputs and 10/5 bp fee/slippage. Decisions monthly; fills next observed session close; valuation/risk daily. Zero-interest cash is primary. Frozen-trade 4% effective annual cash sensitivity is separate. Sharpe retains original .04/252 benchmark and investor-first distribution reporting; matched geometric benchmark is secondary.

## Authority design (explicit policy choice, not an accounting repair)

Monthly household buffer B=max(0,income−mandatory−reserve), as implemented. Every policy receives the same B transfer. Equity purchase approval g=floor_cent(tier_capacity × B), with capacities .25/.50/.75. **Initial purchase authority is zero for every version**: original V1 did not authorize spending starting savings. This isolates carry-forward rather than adding a new initial grant or repeatedly applying tier capacity to all brokerage cash. V1/V3 monthly approval expires before the next month; V2/V4/V5 retain unused approved lots, FIFO, for at most 12 calendar months. Budget=min(unencumbered cash,unspent authority) × existing feedback multiplier. There is no second tier deduction from accumulated authority. Unapproved brokerage cash, including the initial INR 100,000 and the unapproved fraction of B, is not a protected reserve and is not automatically spendable. Dividends/cash-in-lieu do not mint authority. New grants occur once per month; spending including fees consumes lots once. Expiry/revocation removes authorization, not cash. Falling monthly B revokes unused authority before granting the new reduced quota; B=0 suspends purchases. Withdrawals revoke authority up to the withdrawal amount. No income shock automatically liquidates investments.

Report both the original proposal-notional > current B reference and actual newly requested household funding / authority violations. Carrying old approved money may exceed the current-month reference without drawing new household funds. Do not erase that distinction. Key utilization = **externally funded authority spent / unique external approval grants**, with grants counted once, not sum of repeatedly offered balances. Also report original recurring-batch fill, unique brokerage funding deployment, expired/revoked authority, recycled proceeds, cash allocation and remaining authority. No return of sold funds is counted as another external grant.

## Versions

- **V1**: frozen original Full engine, parameters and quantities; reproduced against old summaries/daily ledgers before revised policies. Monthly authority semantics are recorded without changing execution.
- **V2**: carry-forward ledger above; original top-three sector selector, original sizing and held-stock exclusion; original feedback. No new hard position gate is hidden in this carry-only ablation.
- **V3**: original monthly authority, feasibility-aware sizing plus top-ups. Position/sector caps and HHI/risk guard below are explicit additional safety assumptions needed for top-ups.
- **V4**: V2 ledger + V3 construction/safeguards + original feedback.
- **V5** (principal): V4 plus **purchase redirection**. During observed HHI breach, block every sector with equity weight > .25 (rather than only the dominant sector), and require the complete buy basket to strictly reduce HHI. Ranking inputs remain unchanged. No additional decision date or forced spending.
- **V5_SELL** (separate secondary arm, not combined with V5 in main table): V5 plus monthly corrective sales. Greedily remove whole shares from the largest sector only when each removal reduces current HHI; stop at .25, no further reduction, or 10% of current invested value sold. Suppress aggregate sale plans below INR 500. Never sell all holdings to manufacture undefined HHI. Sales fill next-session close before buys with 10/5 bp fee/adverse slippage. Proportional cost basis gives realized P&L; taxes excluded. Net realized sale proceeds are re-approved as **recycled** authority, separately from external household grants. Buy quantities may use only an estimated sale budget at signal and actual funds/authority at fill. Duplicate events cannot execute twice.

## Feasible construction and guards

Use the shared exposure-ascending, historical score-descending, ticker-ascending ranking. No new predictive score or momentum formula. Examine valid ranked candidates for at least one share including estimated fees/slippage; select at most three securities in distinct sectors, supporting fewer. Existing holdings are eligible only in top-up versions. Lower-ranked names can replace infeasible candidates, but do not add a fourth residual name merely to spend money. No extra claim that a negative historical score establishes investment quality; the actual method's history/validity screen is retained.

Seed one feasible share in rank order, then allocate toward the original inverse-volatility weight targets. Binary-search the largest feasible quantity per selected name; distribute a final feasible residual lot among already selected names only. This is a deterministic ranked heuristic, not a claim to solve global integer portfolio optimization. Log when a feasible multi-asset basket might be missed by sequential one-share construction; the measured feasibility diagnostic is a single feasible-share witness, not exhaustive basket feasibility.

For V3/V4/V5 and related construction ablations: resulting single-position value <=20% of total account equity; sector value <=35% of total account equity. If drift already exceeds a cap, do not increase that weight. Equity-only HHI after the complete proposed basket must be <=max(current HHI,.25). An initially empty portfolio is an explicit bootstrap exception: at most three initial sectors cannot achieve HHI<=.25. It is flagged/monitored, never called diversified; position/sector account caps still apply. Subsequent baskets cannot worsen breached HHI and must respect .25 once attained. Same-state hypothetical 30-return volatility and parametric VaR must not cross .03/.10 or worsen an existing breach. CVaR is measured using 252 adjusted returns, not falsely claimed as a hard eligibility limit. Guards include existing plus proposed quantities, costs and cash. No fractional shares, negative holdings or duplicate holding records.

All versions use the source-compatible priority-order whole-share fill cap against actual budget/cash/authority. New guard-enabled versions then revalidate the complete resulting basket using lagged decision risk inputs and current fill prices. Unsafe baskets are cancelled, not silently accepted. V1 retains its original sequential affordability fill exactly; an internal reference-mode parity check assesses shared accounting. The added exposure revalidation is part of the explicit new safety guards. Historical adjusted inputs are revised vendor histories, not point-in-time vintages; no future return observation is read. Existing price/action provenance limitations remain.

## Prespecified ablations and checks

Implementation clarification before revised-policy execution: grandfathered drift-overweight positions/sectors cannot receive additional nominal exposure beyond their existing value; new positions obey caps against post-cost account equity. Fees can therefore marginally increase an unchanged grandfathered position's account fraction. This prevents a fee-only denominator change from prohibiting diversification elsewhere. Fill-time corrective sales are cancelled if current-price HHI does not strictly improve, actual value exceeds the 10% cap after scaling, or the resulting sale plan is below INR 500. Same-state execution counters distinguish changed requested baskets from changed actual trades; only quantity differences and actual selling receive transaction attribution.

Primary five policies × nine profiles × four windows. Secondary V5_SELL × nine primary profiles. Three construction ablations on all nine primary profiles: original construction + new guards, feasible selection without top-ups + guards, and top-ups with original selector + guards. This exposes new-guard confounding in V1/V3 as well as feasibility/top-up interactions. V4 no-momentum zeros only existing momentum coefficients. Household deterioration stress: low/conservative, medium/moderate, high/aggressive; B is zero January–March 2024 for every policy, original B thereafter; no forced withdrawals/sales. Concentrated-start stress: same three profiles and V4/V5/V5_SELL, original 52 ADANIENT shares marked at last pre-start price with remaining cash. These are targeted exploratory stresses, not market holdouts.

Metrics: TWR, CAGR, sample volatility, historical VaR/CVaR95, initial-wealth drawdown, sector HHI conditional on invested equity, valid-session breach frequency, episode lengths/resolution/censoring, position concentration, external authority utilization, top-ups/new positions, costs/turnover. Record internal policy versus fixed-policy same-state baskets, V5 versus V4 direct baskets/actions, actual paired path trade differences and costs. Policy response, changed recommendation and breach resolution are distinct. Moving-block paired uncertainty: 20 sessions, 2,000 draws, seed 20261024. Nine synthetic profiles and overlapping windows are not independent populations; no significance/predictive superiority claim. Exposure-normalized and same-bin comparisons are descriptive with coverage and sparse intervals explicit.

All previous results/source/data are hash-protected. No database connections, live orders, user-account writes, application changes, browser tests or frontend suites. Initial parameter choices stay fixed unless a mathematical implementation defect is documented; any correction preserves the earlier failed attempt and reason.
