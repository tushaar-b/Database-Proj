# Initial root-cause report — before research implementation changes

This report records inspection of existing code and run03 exports. No existing source or baseline result has been changed. Input hashes were frozen first. Findings below distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses requiring event-by-event instrumentation.

## Feedback: restrictive rules and interactions (A/E), not demonstrated propagation failure

There were 55 primary effective policy updates, 0 capacity-multiplier changes, 0 VaR-flagged and 0 volatility-flagged updates. All 432 primary Full decisions recorded zero direct final-basket changes. The research feedback state is passed to propose(), which uses its multiplier and blocked sectors. The application evaluates risk, persists an outbox snapshot, delivers it into recommendation_policy and reads those fields before candidate filtering and sizing. Static inspection confirms meaningful single-portfolio rule agreement; an offline adapter/parity check remains to be run. Application delivery failures are surfaced, not silently ignored.

Every primary update was therefore a sector-policy change rather than an activated capacity reduction. The shared selector already prioritizes sectors with lower exposure, while held stocks are excluded and each batch selects at most three distinct sectors. Blocking the dominant sector may remove candidates that were already held or would not have won the existing exposure ordering. Whole-share filtering occurs only after selecting candidates, so an internal basket change may still leave the final positive-share orders unchanged. Event-level classification will distinguish these cases and transient policy changes with no eligible monthly opportunity. Buy-only feedback has no corrective sell path. Application rebalance is a separately invoked service, not an automatic consequence of feedback.

## Sharpe: correct arithmetic convention, misleading average (B/C/E)

Historical daily r_t = E_t/(E_(t-1)+C_t)-1 with beginning-session contributions; CAGR = product(1+r)^(252/n)-1; sample sd uses ddof=1. Sharpe = sqrt(252)*(mean(r)-.04/252)/sd. The function does not add an epsilon to the denominator: sd<=1e-15 produces NA. The cross-profile table reports the arithmetic mean of defined investor Sharpe ratios, not a pooled or composite portfolio Sharpe. Full mean was -21.801069, median -1.934371, range [-132.191527,0.619130]. The low/conservative ratio is about -132 because almost no funds were invested, cash earned zero, and the benchmark was 4%. These are not by themselves proof of floating-point instability. Independent recalculation, investor-level validity flags and distribution reporting are needed. The linear daily benchmark is a stated convention (effective annual compound rate exceeds 4% slightly); geometric conversion will be separately labelled, not silently substituted.

## Cash: design and restrictive selection (A/B/E)

The reserve is subtracted once in buffer=max(0,income-mandatory-reserve). Only the resulting buffer is contributed; reserve balances are not deposited in brokerage and are not deducted again in replay. Thus 0% of recorded brokerage cash represents a separately held protected reserve. Starting brokerage savings are INR 100,000. Unspent brokerage cash accumulates, but monthly affordability-policy budgets reset to the current monthly buffer times tier capacity (.25/.50/.75), not accumulated cash. This matches the advisory snapshot budget semantics; treating monthly capacity as the only replenishable purchase authorization is an explicit research assumption, not established as an arithmetic bug. The reserve is withheld from each monthly surplus as in the application, rather than modeled as a one-time already-funded reserve target. Discretionary expenses do not reduce the implemented buffer, a documented limitation.

Held-stock exclusion, top-three/distinct-sector selection before whole-share affordability, lack of residual-budget redistribution, tier withholding and ignored accumulated deployment authority can jointly cause underinvestment. Full average brokerage cash was 85.621468%; mean contributed-surplus utilization was 7.956096%. Cash is real unused brokerage money, but not all of it is authorized for a particular monthly order under the original policy. A stage-wise, non-overlapping budget waterfall will quantify this distinction. No double reserve deduction has been found.

## Confirmed reporting defect (D)

HHI is correctly computed from invested equity sector weights and is NA for no holdings; cash is not a sector. Mean HHI skips NA. However hhi_breach_frequency averages Boolean flags over all sessions, implicitly including NA/empty-holdings sessions as nonbreaches. In the primary Full accounts there were 169 undefined HHI observations among 8892 observations. New reporting will divide breach sessions by valid HHI sessions and show holdings coverage explicitly. Original counts/means will be preserved, not overwritten. Existing breach totals do not include undefined sessions; the defect is denominator interpretation, not phantom breach counts.

## Re-evaluation specification fixed before new results

1. Reproduce original primary all four policies (36 runs), with instrumentation; reproduce original concentrated-start No Feedback/Full profiles (6 runs) separately. Same-state old-policy/new-policy and fixed-policy/active-policy counterfactuals will separate immediate effects from path changes. Every policy update receives one primary explanation with optional secondary context.
2. Recalculate Sharpe and HHI from frozen ledgers for all 144 principal profile/policy/window cells. Preserve arithmetic-mean ratios; add median, quantiles, extrema, defined count and a separately labelled equal-capital composite. No denominator clipping or replacement of negative ratios with zero.
3. Cash-interest sensitivity holds every stock transaction and external contribution fixed. Compare 0% and 4% effective annual interest on opening brokerage cash on observed session days, with 252-session geometric conversion. Earnings accrue into a separate unspent interest subledger, so they cannot change stock trades. Report original linear and compounded daily benchmark conventions separately. This is an assumption, not an available guaranteed product.
4. Alternative authorization v1: maintain an explicit approved brokerage cash balance (starting INR 100,000 represents previously approved savings), add only the same reserve-protected monthly buffer, and apply the same tier fraction and feedback multiplier to the accumulated approved cash. No reserve is transferred or disabled; this deliberately changes deployment authority, not fixes a confirmed accounting bug. Keep original no-repeat-stock and ranking rules. Run all four policies and nine profiles; compare versions separately.
5. Exposure control uses paired same-date observations where both policies have strictly positive opening equity exposure, with per-unit invested-capital returns and fixed prespecified exposure bins [0,.05,.25,.50,.75,1]. No forced investments or selling. This is a descriptive exposure-normalization diagnostic, not an executable strategy or causal matching claim. Report common-date coverage, equity-only HHI, conditional tails/drawdown and cost-normalized outcomes. Moving-block paired intervals retain shared dates; no daily independent t-tests.
6. No new trading policy, forced liquidation or live application behavior change is planned. The new directory contains all outputs. Original run03, original conference draft and historical dataset hashes will be verified unchanged at completion.
